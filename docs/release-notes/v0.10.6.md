## Glass Panes v0.10.6 (public beta)

Glass Panes is now documented and organised around the **Lululemon Studio mirror**, the example panes are trimmed to the ones that suit it, and AI agents can build panes for you.

### What changed

- **Three example panes instead of six.** `Mirror`, `House` and `Looping video` stay. `Morning paper`, `Night` and `Off` are gone, because they didn't suit a mirror. You can make your own: **Panes > +**. A blank pane is also how you get a plain mirror, since black is see-through on two-way glass.
- **A display on a removed pane moves to `Mirror`.** When Home Assistant starts, any display still showing `Morning paper`, `Night` or `Off` is put on the `Mirror` pane, and a warning is logged. Panes you saved yourself are never touched. If an automation of yours sets a display to `morning`, `night` or `off`, point it at one of your own panes instead.
- **Agents can build whole panes, widget by widget.** A new Glass Panes tool set shows up in Home Assistant's **Model Context Protocol Server** (and Assist), and the same steps are services. An agent starts a private **draft**, adds, moves, binds and aligns widgets one checked step at a time, then finishes it. The result is a normal pane you can open in the builder and edit by hand. Nothing is shown on a display until you choose it, a step that would break the pane is refused and changes nothing, and finishing never overwrites a pane unless asked. How to connect one: [`docs/agents.md`](https://github.com/git-nimrod/glass-panes/blob/main/docs/agents.md).
- **Two step-by-step guides for the Lululemon Studio mirror:** [part 1, install Android](https://github.com/git-nimrod/glass-panes/blob/main/docs/mirror-app/install-android-lululemon-studio.md), and [part 2, install the Glass Panes app](https://github.com/git-nimrod/glass-panes/blob/main/docs/mirror-app/install-app-lululemon-studio.md). Part 2 now includes a step to test a looping video on your mirror.
- **Which mirrors work.** [`docs/compatibility.md`](https://github.com/git-nimrod/glass-panes/blob/main/docs/compatibility.md#which-mirrors-work) lists the boards and firmware versions people have tried. There's a new **Report your mirror** form on GitHub Issues. If you have a Studio mirror, we'd love to hear how yours goes.
- **Friendlier docs throughout**, and the builder screenshots in the README and Getting started.
- **A safety net for releases:** app and Android image releases that are accidentally published as normal releases are turned back into pre-releases automatically, so HACS never offers them as a Glass Panes update.

### Install or update

HACS > Glass Panes > **Download**, then **restart Home Assistant** (this release changes Python). Updating keeps your panes, uploads and logins. After the restart, the three removed panes disappear from every display's pane list.

### Known limits

- The vinyl and CD turn speeds are a first guess and haven't been tuned on a real mirror yet.
- Only one Lululemon Studio unit (RevP21, software 1.25.0) has been tested by the project. Please report yours.
- Agents place widgets by their saved numbers and can't see a rendered preview yet, so look at what an agent builds before you show it on the mirror.
- The point-and-click editor for warnings is not built yet; use services for now.
- Only English so far.
