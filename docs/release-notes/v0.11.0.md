## Glass Panes v0.11.0 (public beta)

> Wow! Thank you for the feedback and for testing on all of your units. This is another beta build with some new features, better logging, and stability and ease-of-use updates for the associated web app. It's much easier to install if you already had the previous .apk on your mirror: download the new .apk, install it over the old one, and update the integration in HACS. Steps are in the [update guide](https://github.com/git-nimrod/glass-panes/blob/main/docs/mirror-app/update-app.md). Please give me some more feedback and show me what you've been building! This is really exciting, and I'm hoping to keep supporting it.

Photos, music through the mirror's own speaker, a Settings page, new clock looks and a better builder.

### What changed

**Photos and pictures**
- **A photo slideshow widget:** add Photo slideshow from Facets, type a folder inside one of Home Assistant's media folders (for example `photos/family`), and the pane shows the photos one after another. You choose how long each stays (5 seconds to a day), shuffle or file-name order, fit the whole photo or fill the box, and fade or switch instantly. Network storage you have added to Home Assistant appears under `/media`, so a folder on a NAS works too.
- **Big photos no longer overload a small mirror:** Home Assistant shrinks each photo to the widget's size the first time it is needed, keeps the small copy (up to 300 MB, oldest removed first), and sends only that. The page holds just two pictures at a time, however big the folder is.
- **Pictures from image and camera entities:** the Image widget can be bound to an `image.*` or `camera.*` entity (a doorbell snapshot, a weather radar picture, a media server's photo frame) and shows the picture instead of the entity's state ("Idle", "On" or a time). A camera refreshes every 10 seconds by default and an image entity when it changes; the `refresh_s` option changes that. The old Entity value widget shows the picture too, so existing panes that showed "Idle" are fixed by updating.

**Music**
- **The mirror's speaker is a media player:** with Mirror Kiosk app 0.11.0, a display that has its mirror connected gets a speaker, `media_player.glass_panes_<display>_speaker`. Play, pause, stop, seek and volume work from automations, scripts and dashboard cards, and Music Assistant can play to it, which brings Spotify, Plex and other services with it. Only audio from `http` and `https` addresses is played. The speaker appears when you connect a mirror and disappears when you disconnect it.
- **Now playing shows the song:** when Music Assistant plays to the mirror, the speaker shows the title, artist, album, cover and length of the song, and follows song changes straight away.
- **The CD look is a printed CD:** in Now playing, choose Look: CD. The album cover covers the whole disc, with a shine and a clear ring round the hole, and the disc turns while a song plays. A new checkbox, CD case beside the disc, also draws the cover as a CD case with the disc sliding out of its side. The vinyl look is unchanged.

**Clock and date**
- **Three clock looks:** the Clock widget now has a Look: digital numbers (the original), a traditional round clock face, or a bedside alarm clock. The two round looks have a light or dark face, and Show seconds adds a red second hand.
- **A time zone for each clock and date:** type a place such as `America/Chicago` or `Asia/Tokyo` in the widget's Time zone box, or leave it empty for the display's own zone.

**The builder**
- **A Settings page:** the gear next to Dark mode opens a page for the mirror. It has a Speaker card (what is playing, volume, pause, resume, stop), presence on and off with its settings, brightness, the memory the mirror reports, and the mirror's address. It used to sit inside the Displays tab, where it was hard to find. A Planned card lists the Bluetooth proxy and the voice assistant and says they are not built yet.
- **A calmer, bigger canvas:** while you edit, the pane list steps aside so the canvas is larger. The pane settings sit in one row. The toolbar groups its buttons so Save never ends up alone on its own line, and on a phone Save comes right after the editing tools. The delete button shows only on the widget you picked. The phone and tablet layouts no longer cut the canvas off or push the page sideways.
- **A tidier inspector:** the side panel starts with the basics: name, what the widget shows, which entity feeds it, text size and colour, opacity, position and size. The rules (Show when and layout) fold away until needed and open by themselves when a widget already uses one. The move, copy and delete icons are now labelled buttons.

**Also**
- **Sports:** a game later today says Today before 5 pm and Tonight from 5 pm (in the display's own time zone).
- This release includes everything since v0.10.10, among it the "Showing" strip and tags, and the option to keep true black behind a dark theme (v0.10.11).

### Fixed

- **Display settings said "answered but not like the Mirror Kiosk app", and the speaker stayed unavailable:** the mirror app sends its status in more than one piece, and only the first piece was being read, so the answer looked cut off. It is now read to the end.
- **Cameras and image entities showed "Idle":** they now show the picture (see above).
- **Confirmation messages and dialogs went missing** while the Settings page was open. They now show on every page.
- **The Settings page ran off the edge of a phone screen:** it now fits.
- **Reloading the panel after an update logged an "already been used" error in the browser:** it no longer does.
- **Layout problems in the builder's toolbar and on small screens,** listed under The builder above.

### Install or update

HACS > Glass Panes > the three dots > **Redownload**, pick **v0.11.0**, then **restart Home Assistant** (Settings > System > the power icon > Restart Home Assistant), because this version adds new parts that Home Assistant only learns at start-up. Then reload the builder page once (Ctrl+F5). Your panes, uploads and displays are kept.

The speaker and the Settings page's Speaker card need Mirror Kiosk app 0.11.0 on the mirror. Everything else works with the app you have. See [compatibility.md](../compatibility.md), and the [update guide](https://github.com/git-nimrod/glass-panes/blob/main/docs/mirror-app/update-app.md) for the app itself.

To play Music Assistant music on the mirror, open Music Assistant, go to Settings > Providers, add the **Home Assistant** plug-in if it is not there, then add the **Home Assistant Media Players** provider and tick only the mirror's speaker. It then appears in Music Assistant's Players list. Menu names can differ between Music Assistant versions.

### Known limits

- The slideshow and picture widgets work in the automated tests, in the builder preview and on one setup with a few photos. They have not been tried on a large folder or a real camera feed.
- The speaker plays what Android 6 on the mirror can decode (MP3, AAC, FLAC, Ogg Vorbis and WAV are the safe ones). Music Assistant playing to it has been tried on one mirror, with one service. Music Assistant calls it a "Home Assistant media player" and describes that as a fallback: the mirror cannot be grouped to play in sync with other speakers.
- The song title and cover shown on the speaker are copied from Music Assistant's own copy of the speaker, so they appear only when Music Assistant is the one playing.
- Home Assistant's own files are sent to the mirror as an address on Home Assistant's local URL. Keep that a plain `http://` address on your home network, because Android 6 does not trust many modern HTTPS certificates.
- The Settings page, the new clock looks and the CD case have been checked in the builder preview at phone, tablet and desktop sizes, and the CD look on one mirror. The clock looks and the case have not been judged on a real mirror yet.
- The Bluetooth proxy and the voice assistant do not exist yet. The [roadmap](https://github.com/git-nimrod/glass-panes/blob/main/docs/roadmap.md) says what is planned.
