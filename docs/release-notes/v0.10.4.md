## Glass Panes v0.10.4 (public beta)

### What changed
- **Builder toolbar in a half-width window.** The toolbar at the bottom of the preview (Add, undo, Demo data, Mirror view, Discard, Save) used to slide sideways with the scroll bar hidden, so **Save** could end up out of sight. It now wraps onto a second row and everything stays on screen.
- **Selecting a widget that is covered by another.** An empty widget (for example Now playing with nothing playing) is easily covered by the widget that slid up into its place, so it could not be clicked. Click a widget again, or Alt-click, to select the one underneath, and keep going to step through them all. Hover a box for the reminder. Then, in its side panel, **Preview with sample data** shows what it looks like.

### Install or update
HACS > Glass Panes > Download. No restart is needed (only the builder's script and style changed). Updating keeps your panes, uploads and logins.

**Mirror app:** version number only (0.10.4), no code change. Keep what you have unless you want the matching number.

### Known limits
- The ready-made Android images for the mirror are not published yet.
- Fonts are a small bundled set; formal fonts and pane colour themes are next.
- Only English so far.
