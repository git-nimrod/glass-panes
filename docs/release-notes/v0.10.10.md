## Glass Panes v0.10.10 (public beta)

More control over how text looks, and widgets that fade instead of popping.

### What changed

- **Every widget option can be set in the builder.** Select a widget and open **More options**: everything that widget can do is there, with a short explanation under each. That includes the clock's 24-hour and AM/PM settings, every date and weather option, calendar days and time style, how many to-do items, Now playing's idle text, avatar size, status and progress ring settings, sports layout and news filters. Blank means "use the default", so pane files stay tidy.
- **More text styling in the builder.** Select a widget and the inspector now has Italic, ALL CAPS, letter spacing, line height, any colour (a colour picker next to the named colours) and opacity. Opacity also works on pictures and dividers.
- **Pane background and brightness.** While editing, the toolbar has a Background colour (used when no theme is chosen; pure black is see-through on the mirror) and a Brightness choice (the backlight while this pane shows, or "As is").
- **Fade.** Tick **Fade** in the toolbar and widgets that "show when" rules switch on and off fade in and out over about half a second. New panes start with it on; your existing panes stay exactly as they were until you tick it. Agents can set it too (`set_pane` with `fade`). Displays that ask for reduced motion never fade.
- **Burn-in drift for a whole pane.** Tick **Burn-in drift** in the toolbar and the entire pane wanders a few pixels over about 40 minutes, so a mirror left on all day never holds the same pixels. Too slow to notice. (It stays still in the builder so the outlines line up.)
- **Easier editing.** While you drag a widget, its real content moves with the outline, so you can line it up against what is really there. The toolbar at the bottom no longer covers the lower part of the pane. Widgets with no name show what they are (`Text: "SPORTS"` instead of "text" eight times), on the canvas and in the "Place below" and "Show when" lists. New **Bring forward** and **Send back** buttons (the up and down arrows next to Duplicate) decide which widget is on top where two overlap.
- **A heads-up before heavy uploads.** Before a picture or video is uploaded, the builder checks it in your browser and asks first if it could weigh the mirror down: pictures over about 4 megapixels, big animated GIFs, WebM videos, videos bigger than 1080p or with a very high bitrate. You can still upload it.
- **New how-to: a mirror that wakes up when you walk up** (in Getting started): presence, a toggle, "show when" and Fade.
- **Videos are drawn honestly.** A pane with a video is marked in the builder as playing full screen, because that is what the mirror does, whatever size the box is.
- **Includes v0.10.9** (the self-healing connection, start retry and stuck-page heartbeat), for anyone skipping it.
- **Calendars recover more calmly.** A calendar that fails to load is retried after 15 seconds, then 30 s, 1 min and 2 min, then waits for the normal 5-minute refresh. One that Home Assistant refuses (deleted, or no access) is not hammered. A retry left over from a pane you switched away from no longer keeps running in the background.

### Known limits

- The new controls have been checked in the builder preview, not yet on the tested unit's own screen.
- Fade applies to show-when switching only. Fading between whole panes is planned.

### Install or update

HACS > Glass Panes > **Download**, then restart Home Assistant (Settings > System > the power icon > Restart Home Assistant), because the agent tools changed. Reload the mirror's page once: `http://<mirror address>:8099/reload`.
