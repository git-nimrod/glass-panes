## Glass Panes v0.10.9 (public beta)

The mirror now heals itself after network and Home Assistant hiccups.

### What changed

- **A frozen connection is noticed.** If Home Assistant stops answering (its computer freezes, the router restarts, Wi-Fi drops) the player notices within about a minute and reconnects, instead of showing frozen values for 15 minutes or more. Its keep-alive checks also no longer stop working after 8 hours on one connection.
- **A failed start is retried.** If the mirror starts before Home Assistant is ready (after a power cut, for example), the player tries again calmly (10 seconds, growing to 5 minutes) instead of staying on the wrong pane or a blank page.
- **The mirror app can tell when the page is stuck.** With mirror app 0.10.6, the player says "I'm alive" every 30 seconds, and the app reloads a page that never starts (such as Home Assistant's "404: Not Found" while it is booting) or that freezes.
- The builder's demo data no longer replaces a message widget's own lines in the preview.

### Install or update

HACS > Glass Panes > **Download**. No restart needed. Reload the mirror's page once: `http://<mirror address>:8099/reload`. For the stuck-page protection, also install mirror app 0.10.6.
