## Glass Panes v0.10.5 (public beta)

This one has a lot in it: new looks, new ways to decide what shows when, and a settings panel for your mirror. Restart Home Assistant after updating (see below).

### What's new

- **Widgets that switch on and off.** Click a widget and look for **Show when**. Make it depend on another widget having something to show (or nothing), a door or other entity being in a state, a time of day, or certain days of the week. The classic use: a photo fills the spot when no team plays today. You choose whether the gap closes (the default: things below slide up) or keeps its space. Entities never go in the pane itself, so exports and shipped panes stay clean. Details: [`docs/pane-schema.md`](https://github.com/git-nimrod/glass-panes/blob/main/docs/pane-schema.md#show-when-widgets-that-switch-on-and-off).
- **Now playing, vinyl and CD.** Pick a **Look**: the original card, a black vinyl record with a tonearm that swings on when the music starts, or a silver CD. Nothing moves unless a song is playing, and everything stays cheap enough for a small mirror.
- **Burn-in saver for Now playing.** A switch that drifts the block a few pixels over 40 minutes, far too slowly to see, so a screen left on all day never holds the same pixels. Off by default.
- **Six pane themes.** Moss, Cream, **Cream Night**, Midnight, Paper and Slate recolour a whole pane in one choice. Cream Night is the cream palette on a very dark warm background, made for two-way mirror glass where only dark is see-through.
- **Five more fonts** for a formal look: Cormorant Garamond, Playfair Display, Montserrat, Lora and DM Serif Display. They only download when a pane uses them.
- **Display settings for the Mirror Kiosk app.** In **Displays**, connect a mirror by its address to see whether the page loaded, switch presence detection on and off, change its settings in plain words, set brightness, and read the memory the mirror reports. **Measure what presence costs** asks you first. Home Assistant talks to the mirror, not your browser, and the mirror's key is never shown or returned to anything, including agents and automations.
- **Mirror app releases are now separate.** The app has its own releases (`app-v...`) and the integration keeps `v0.10.N`, so you never have to touch your mirror to get a builder update. Which app version a feature needs: [`docs/compatibility.md`](https://github.com/git-nimrod/glass-panes/blob/main/docs/compatibility.md). Nothing in this release needs a newer app, and this release carries no app file.
- **Ready-made Android images** for the Lululemon mirror are now published as their own pre-release (`mirror-android-1`), with checksums. Step-by-step guide: [`docs/mirror-app/install-android.md`](https://github.com/git-nimrod/glass-panes/blob/main/docs/mirror-app/install-android.md).
- **Guides** for the mirror got a clean-up, including one clear way to use the project's ADB key.

### Install or update

HACS > Glass Panes > **Download**, then **restart Home Assistant**. Display settings and the show-when rules add new Python code, which Home Assistant only loads at startup. Updating keeps your panes, uploads and logins.

### Known limits

- The vinyl and CD turn speeds (8 and 6 seconds per turn) are a first guess and have not been tuned on a real mirror yet.
- Show-when rules, display settings and the new looks are tested against a real Home Assistant core and in the builder preview, and are still being settled in daily use. Please tell us what looks off.
- Show-when has some deliberate limits: an entity that is not bound yet never makes a rule hold (the widget stays off), day and time are separate conditions (no "Friday 22:00 to 02:00"), and a looping video cannot take a rule.
- The point-and-click editor for warnings (doors, cameras, litter and so on) is not built yet; use services for now.
- Only English so far.
