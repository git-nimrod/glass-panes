## Glass Panes v0.10.8 (public beta)

A reliability release for pictures, videos and logins on the mirror.

### What changed

- **Pictures and videos survive a Home Assistant restart.** An uploaded file's link used to stop working every time Home Assistant restarted (and after 7 days), leaving a blank spot or a black video. The player now fetches a fresh link when one fails, when it reconnects to Home Assistant, and every 6 hours. A failing video retries calmly (30 seconds, then up to 10 minutes apart) instead of in a tight loop.
- **No more flood of failed logins.** When Home Assistant refuses a display's login, the player stops asking and shows its sign-in screen. Before, its widgets kept trying, and each try counted as a failed login (with a ban threshold set, Home Assistant could ban the mirror).

### Install or update

HACS > Glass Panes > **Download**. No restart needed (only the player changed). Reload the mirror's page once: `http://<mirror address>:8099/reload`.

### Known limits

- A video playing in the mirror app picks up a fresh link within about 30 seconds of Home Assistant coming back.
