## Glass Panes v0.13.0 (public beta)

This is the biggest update yet! Thank you again for your interest and your feedback. The mirror can now talk back with Home Assistant's voice assistant, with its own wake word, can work as a Bluetooth proxy, and can turn on and off by itself on a schedule that you set. Along with these, I've gone through and added new widgets, fonts and looks.

This also went through a deep UX and security review, in addition to making it easier to install.

I'm always looking for more feedback, and I very much appreciate everyone who has joined the Discord, and especially @aguilaair for the help.

Next, I'm targeting more custom widgets, the ability to add your own widgets to a broader inventory shared between users, and support for the VPY model. Once we get VPY, this will be considered fully released!

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Everything new on the mirror is **off until you switch it on**. Nothing runs, listens or downloads for a feature you haven't turned on or put on a pane.

> [!NOTE]
> The mirror features need **Mirror app 0.13.0**. Voice, Bluetooth and self-updates work best on the new **`mirror-android-3`** image, which has the app built in. "Which version do I need" below has the full breakdown.

### What's new, quickly

- **Voice assistant and wake word** ("Okay Nabu", "Hey Jarvis" or "Hey Mycroft"), running on the mirror itself
- **Bluetooth proxy** for nearby sensors and trackers
- **Screen off for real**, plus quiet hours, so the panel goes fully dark overnight
- **App updates from Home Assistant**, with no cable
- **A reworked editor** with named looks, a widget catalog and editing text right on the canvas
- **New widgets:** news feeds, photo backgrounds, radar, the moon, planes overhead, Monet's water lilies, sparkles, ASCII art, scorebugs and 57 lookalike fonts

### Setting up a mirror

The builder now talks about **mirrors**, since that's what Glass Panes is for. There's a **Mirrors** tab, and **Add mirror** opens straight on the pairing form, so you just type in the six-digit code and the address the mirror shows on its screen. The old sign-in link is still there under "No code on the mirror?" if your mirror app is older than 0.12.

Home Assistant's address is tucked under **Advanced**, because the mirror usually reaches Home Assistant at its normal address anyway. Each mirror's card starts with how it's connected, so the next step is always right there. After pairing, the builder waits up to a minute for the mirror's page to load and tells you how it went, and a wrong code keeps what you typed.

### New controls for each mirror in Home Assistant

These show up on each mirror's Glass Panes device. They're ordinary Home Assistant entities like every other Glass Panes setting, so you can use them in automations.

| Entity | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Screen** (switch) | Turns the panel fully off and back on: dark glass, no backlight glow. The page is not reloaded while the screen is off. | On |
| **Screen mode** (select) | **Manual** (the Screen switch is yours), **Auto (quiet hours)**, **Always on**, **Always off** | Manual |
| **Quiet hours start / end** (time) | When the screen is off in Auto. Uses Home Assistant's time zone. | 23:00 to 07:00 |
| **Mirror app** (update) | Shows when a newer Mirror app is released, and installs it on a mirror that has the app built in | |
| **Bluetooth proxy** (switch) | The mirror passes nearby Bluetooth devices (sensors, trackers) to Home Assistant | **Off** |
| **Voice** (switch) | The mirror becomes a Home Assistant voice assistant, with its own microphone and speaker | **Off** |
| **Wake word** (switch) | The mirror listens for "Okay Nabu", "Hey Jarvis" or "Hey Mycroft" by itself; needs Voice on | **Off** |

There's also a new blueprint that makes the mirror's brightness follow a light sensor. It changes slowly on a smooth curve and only touches the Brightness number.

### Voice and Bluetooth proxy

Setup takes no typing. Switch on Voice or Bluetooth proxy, and within a minute **Settings > Devices & services** shows a **Discovered** card for the mirror under ESPHome. Click **Add**, then **Submit**. Home Assistant makes an encryption key and hands it to the mirror itself, so you never see or type one, and the link is encrypted from then on.

Voice works like any other Home Assistant voice satellite: you pick its assistant on the new ESPHome device. Answers play through the mirror's speaker, and announcements (`assist_satellite.announce`) work too.

The wake word runs on the mirror. The three models are inside the app, nothing gets downloaded, and microphone audio only goes to Home Assistant after the wake word is heard. A small dot in the corner shows when it's listening for the wake word, **"Listening…"** shows while it records your question, then **"Thinking…"**.

It's light on the hardware. On the tested mirror the wake word uses about 3% of one CPU core, and free memory stayed at 270 MB with Bluetooth, Voice and Wake word all on. Switching any of them off stops it right away and frees what it was using. With all three off, the mirror runs no Bluetooth or voice code at all.

### The editor

- **Looks, basics and pane settings are reorganized.** You get one-click looks for a widget, the common settings first, and a **This pane** section for anything that applies to the whole pane.
- **A Widgets catalog with live previews** is now inside the edit area, and it tells you what each widget needs ("works with a weather entity").
- **You can edit text right on the canvas**, and resizing a text widget scales its font.
- **Show a draft on the mirror for two minutes** before you save it.
- **Warnings can run only at certain times**, like warning you if the porch light is on between 01:00 and 04:00.
- **A pane this version can't read is kept, never erased.** If a saved pane uses a widget or font from a newer or customized build, it's set aside instead of loaded, and saving your other panes won't delete it anymore.

### Pictures and data

- **Photo backgrounds:** one photo or a slideshow from a Home Assistant media folder behind a pane's widgets, with an optional dark veil so text stays readable.
- **News feed widget:** headlines from any RSS or Atom feed. Home Assistant fetches the feed and keeps a copy, so the mirror never contacts the news site, and the mirror only redraws when the stories change. News can also rotate through headlines.
- **Weather radar:** from the web, plus the US color composite.
- **Moon phase:** real NASA photos of the moon, plus sensors from Home Assistant's Moon integration. It lives in a new **Sky & stars** group.
- **Planes overhead:** where the nearest plane is going, its airline and flight, the aircraft, and its height, speed and distance, plus a small radar.

### Fun widgets, fonts and looks

All of these load only when a pane uses them, so a mirror that doesn't place them pays nothing for them.

- **Lily pond:** pieces cut from Claude Monet's *Water Lilies* (public domain), arranged fresh each day. **Art pond** does the same thing with your own cut-outs.
- **Sparkles:** a decoration across the whole screen, with star shapes and color sets.
- **Anime-inspired looks:** falling petals, "kira" glints, speech bubbles and a chibi weather face.
- **ASCII art** widget.
- **Clocks:** stacked and sideways layouts, and a **Galactic** look with an alien-alphabet font, plus a Galactic speech bubble.
- **Fonts:** 57 free lookalike fonts with the feel of famous paid typefaces and screen styles, plus **Aurebesh** (a fan-made alphabet) and **Galactic**. Every font ships with its license file.
- **Sports looks:** Scorebug, Big score, Compact and One line. I've found nobody is really doing scorebugs, so there are **broadcast-style scorebugs** for NFL, NBA, NHL and MLB, with retro boxes and an optional old-TV picture. Team and league logos load from ESPN's public logo service when they're shown, and no league, team or network artwork ships with Glass Panes. The network's spot shows the Home Assistant logo, because these are tributes and not the real broadcasters' graphics.

### How this release was reviewed

I wanted v0.13 checked properly before it went out, so it went through four reviews. Open each one for the details.

<details>
<summary><b>UX review</b> of the builder, mirror pairing and the agent path</summary>

<br>

This was an expert walkthrough of the builder, the pairing flow on both the mirror and Home Assistant, and what an AI agent sees through the Glass Panes tools. The main thing it found was that connecting a mirror was spread over three overlapping paths in two places, using five different names for the same things. v0.13 fixes that:

- there's one front door, **Add mirror**, and it starts with the code on the mirror's screen;
- the networking field moved behind "Advanced";
- the same words are used everywhere: Mirror, Mirrors, Add mirror, Pair with a code, Settings, Pane, Facets.

The review also set a "definition of done" for anything a person sees, and every new screen in v0.13 follows it:

- one obvious place to find it;
- every state covered: empty, loading, an error with a plain reason, success, and "this needs a newer mirror app";
- checked at phone width as well as desktop;
- reachable by agents;
- anything you might automate is a Home Assistant entity.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Design critique and plain-words review</b> of the new widgets</summary>

<br>

The planes and moon widgets got reworked after a critique:

- the plane card leads with where the plane is going;
- heights are always in feet (the "FL" aviation jargon is gone), and speeds are in mph or km/h, never knots;
- the radar labels don't overlap anymore;
- messages are plain sentences, like "No planes nearby right now" and "Full moon on Tuesday".

<details>
<summary><b>Secure-coding review</b> against the OWASP checklist</summary>

<br>

Each area was checked against the OWASP Secure Coding Practices:

- **Input:** uploads are checked by what's actually in the file, not its name, and only real images and videos get accepted (no SVG). Uploads have size limits, and file paths can't reach outside their folders. Feeds are capped in size, and feeds that declare their own XML entities are refused.
- **Who can do what:** everything that changes something needs a Home Assistant admin. The news feed address is read from the saved pane and never from the request, so a display's login can't make Home Assistant fetch an address of its choosing.
- **Output:** entity and feed text is shown as plain text and never run as code.
- **The mirror's control port:**
  - its key is checked in a way that can't be guessed one character at a time;
  - web pages from other sites are refused;
  - wrong pairing codes lock pairing for a while;
  - **an unpaired mirror now answers only pairing and status requests** (new in app 0.13.0);
  - an app update only installs if its fingerprint matches, it's signed with the project's release key, and it's a newer version.
- **Secrets:** none in the code or the logs.
- **CI:** a static security scanner (bandit) now runs on every change.

</details>

### Which version do I need

| What you want | Integration | Mirror app | Image |
|---|---|---|---|
| Everything in the editor, widgets, fonts, news feeds, photo backgrounds | v0.13.0 | any | any |
| Screen switch, Screen mode, quiet hours | v0.13.0 | 0.13.0 | any (screen off needs one `adb` command on older images) |
| Bluetooth proxy, Voice, Wake word | v0.13.0 | 0.13.0 | any (`mirror-android-3` grants the microphone and location for you) |
| App updates installed from Home Assistant | v0.13.0 | 0.13.0 | **`mirror-android-3`** (the app must be built in) |

### Install or update

1. HACS > Glass Panes > the three dots > **Redownload**, pick **v0.13.0**, then **restart Home Assistant** (Python changed).
2. Update the mirror app to 0.13.0 (see the Mirror app 0.13.0 release), or flash `mirror-android-3`, which has it built in.
3. Reload the builder page.

Your panes, displays and bindings carry over. The new mirror switches start **off** and Screen mode starts on **Manual**, so nothing changes until you change it.

### Known limits

- **If the assistant can't answer something, that's usually the assistant and not the mirror.** Home Assistant's built-in assistant controls your devices and tells you the local time. For weather it needs a weather entity exposed to Assist. General questions ("time in Japan", jokes) need an AI conversation agent, which you choose in your assistant's settings. Starting a conversation from an automation (`assist_satellite.start_conversation`) also needs an AI agent. Announcements work with any agent.
- **Hearing from across a room:** the mirror's microphone works best within a few meters. It uses the voice-call microphone mode, which was the clearest on the tested unit.
- **False wakes over a long evening with TV or music haven't been counted yet.** If the mirror wakes up by itself too often, switch Wake word off. Voice still works when Home Assistant starts it.
- **Memory depends on the pane.** On the tested mirror, Glass Panes used 250 to 290 MB depending on what was showing.
- **The mirror's control key and the pairing code travel as plain `http` on your home network.** Moving them out of the address is planned.
- **Installing app updates from Home Assistant** needs the app built into the image (`mirror-android-3`). On older images the update entity tells you a new app is out, and you install it with `adb`. The first real update through Home Assistant will be the first app release after 0.13.0.
- **The newer NXP-board mirrors** (FCC ID `VPYLBEE5HY1MW`) aren't supported yet. See the compatibility page.
- This was tested on one mirror (a RevP21). Please tell us how yours goes with the "Report your mirror" issue form, and don't post your mirror's serial number.
