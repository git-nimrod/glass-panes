## Glass Panes v0.12.0 (public beta)

Headlines that say something, and a sports option for narrow columns.

### What changed

- **News: teaser headlines and video items are skipped.** A headline on a mirror has to make sense at a glance, so the News widget now drops headlines that only invite you into the article ("ERs are losing money. Here's why", "What it means for you", "The hidden cost of ..."), and items that are really a video, a livestream, audio or a photo gallery (WATCH:, LISTEN:, "in photos" ...). A headline that states a number stays even when it starts like a label. This joins the existing rules that skip questions, explainers and very long headlines.
- **Sports: "Headline above the logos".** A new option for the rows layout (`head_top`, in the widget's More options) puts Final, Live or "Today · 7 PM" above the two logos instead of beside them, so a game fits a narrow column.

### Mirror app

Mirror app 0.12.0 is released alongside this as its own pre-release. It fixes a loading screen that could stay up over a working page after a slow Wi-Fi start, and a pointless app restart after a power cut. Nothing in this integration release needs it, but it is recommended for everyone. See [compatibility](https://github.com/git-nimrod/glass-panes/blob/main/docs/compatibility.md).

### Known limits

- The headline filter is a set of English wording rules. It will now and then drop a fine headline or keep a teaser; tell us which ones.
- The headline-above option has been seen on the tested unit's mirror in a narrow column only.
