# Monet water lilies (Lily pond widget)

Cut-out pieces (lily pads, flowers and grass) from Claude Monet's *Water Lilies* paintings. Claude Monet (1840-1926) died more
than 70 years ago and the paintings are in the public domain.

`sizes.json` lists each picture's width, height and kind (`pad`, `flower` or `grass`); the Lily pond widget arranges them anew each day.
