GlassPanes

Let’s sign this display in to Home Assistant. It only takes a moment, and only once.

  1. 1
    The easy way. In Home Assistant open Glass Panes, go to Displays, press Set up login on this display, and open the link it gives you here.
  2. 2
    Or by hand. Click your name at the bottom of Home Assistant’s sidebar, open Security, scroll to Long-lived access tokens, press Create token, copy it and paste it below.

The login is saved only on this device.
